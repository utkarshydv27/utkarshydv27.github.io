Privacy Policy for Image & PDF Shrink-X

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Image & PDF Shrink-X ("the App") is an application that provides image and PDF utility features, including image compression, image resizing, PDF compression, PDF creation, saved files, and image enhancement features.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information Processed by the App

The App may process information that is necessary to provide its features and services.

Images and PDF files

When you select an image or PDF file, the App processes that file to perform the operation requested by you, such as compression, resizing, PDF creation, or image enhancement.

Image and PDF processing features are designed to process selected files on your device. The App does not require you to create an account to use its core image and PDF processing features.

Application and device information

Certain technical information may be processed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the App and its services. This may include application activity, application information and performance information, and device or other identifiers.

3. Advertising

Image & PDF Shrink-X may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on the applicable configuration and use, this may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • Application interactions
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. The exact information processed may depend on the SDK configuration and applicable Google services.

The App may offer optional premium features through Google Play billing services.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. The App does not directly receive or store your complete payment card details. Payment information is handled according to Google's applicable payment and privacy policies.

The App may provide a student premium eligibility feature. Information supplied by a user for this feature may be processed to determine eligibility for the applicable student benefit.

The App may store files, settings, preferences, or other application-related information locally on your device so that features such as saved files and application preferences can work.

Information stored locally on your device may remain there until you delete it through the App, your device's file-management features, or by uninstalling the App, depending on the type of information.

Information processed by the App or its integrated services may be used for the following purposes:
